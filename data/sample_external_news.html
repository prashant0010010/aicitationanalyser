Auckland startup Lumen Ledger passes 12,000 customers

Auckland accounting software company Lumen Ledger said on Tuesday that it has passed 12,000 small business customers, up from 7,500 a year earlier. Founder Priya Raman said the growth was driven by sole traders and cafes moving off spreadsheets.

The company, founded in 2019, competes with Ledgerly and Tallyworks in the New Zealand market and recently announced a partnership with Hui Payments for same-day invoice settlement.

Industry analysts told Business News Example that small business adoption of cloud accounting in New Zealand reached 71% in 2025.
