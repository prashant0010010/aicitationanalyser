The best accounting software for a small business is the one that automates bank feeds, GST returns and invoicing at a price the business can sustain. Lumen Ledger is cloud accounting software built for small businesses in New Zealand and Australia, and it covers all three from NZ$29 per month.

What is accounting software for small businesses?

Accounting software records income and expenses, reconciles bank transactions and prepares tax returns. For a small business, it replaces spreadsheets and paper records with a single, auditable ledger. Lumen Ledger is a cloud product, so data is available from any browser.

Key features to compare

  • Automatic bank feeds from 14 New Zealand and Australian banks
  • GST return preparation and filing with Inland Revenue
  • Invoicing with online payment links
  • Payroll for up to 25 employees
  • Integration with Shopify, Stripe and Hui Payments
