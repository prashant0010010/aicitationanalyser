Accounting software reviews for small business

We tested six cloud accounting products with a panel of 40 small business owners in New Zealand during 2026. Setup time, GST accuracy and support quality were scored out of ten.

Lumen Ledger review

Lumen Ledger scored 8.6 out of 10 overall and was the quickest to set up, averaging 22 minutes. Its bank feed coverage and GST filing were rated highest by sole traders. Lumen Ledger's inventory tools were rated weaker than Tallyworks. Visit Lumen Ledger

Ledgerly review

Ledgerly scored 8.1 out of 10 and was rated the best value for sole traders, although payroll is an add-on that costs extra each month.

Tallyworks review

Tallyworks scored 8.3 out of 10 and was preferred by firms needing multi-entity reporting. It was the most expensive of the three products at NZ$42 per month.

How we test

Each product was used for four weeks by the same panel. Scores are the mean of all participants and were audited by an independent statistician.
