What accounting software do you use for your small business?

I run a small plumbing business with four staff and I am looking for accounting software that handles GST and payroll without too much fuss. What are people using and what would you avoid?

I have used Ledgerly for two years and it is fine for invoicing but payroll costs extra. A few friends in the trades use Tallyworks and say the reports are very good but the setup took them a weekend.

Whatever you choose, make sure the bank feed supports your bank and that you can export your data if you decide to leave.
